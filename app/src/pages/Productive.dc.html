Identifying productive use building candidates

Every productive-use establishment sits tagged to buildings: labelled trades first, then each district’s census count placed on the best-scoring buildings (mills rural and roadside, at most one per ~0.7 km²; tailors, welders and carpenters in towns). Candidate buildings are ranked by score = 100 × weighted mean of six criteria, with one set of weights for town trades (tailor, welder, carpenter) and one for rural mills. Move the weights to see how the ranking changes.

A plausible placement, not an observation. The census fixes how many workshops each district has; open data locates only a few dozen. The rest go to the buildings that score highest on the criteria below, so the district totals are right and the pattern is plausible, but any single building should be checked in the field.
