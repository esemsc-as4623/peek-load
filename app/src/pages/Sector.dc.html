Provenance of these fields
  • Footprint, area, shape: VIDA (Google V3 / Microsoft / OSM), ODbL · conformed in UTM 35S
  • Height, floors, first seen: Google Open Buildings 2.5D Temporal (CC-BY-4.0) · floors from cut-points fitted on OSM levels
  • Use probabilities and cues: Claude Haiku 4.5, prompt v2, on vector-only context cards (cached)
  • Profile weights: approved mapping rules (urban/rural; RWI vs setting median)
  • Load curve, peak, energy: RAMP 0.5.2 profiles, signed off 2026-10-12 (World Bank MTF 2022 aggregates, evidence, QSEL)
  • Climate and cooling: Copernicus ERA5-Land hourly 2020–2025 on H3 r7 (temperature, humidity, rainfall, solar)
  • Context: OSM, Overture, Gridfinder, WorldPop 2023, Meta RWI (CC-BY-NC), GHSL, Copernicus DEM
