Licences in brief

  • Footprints and OSM data: ODbL-1.0. Derived databases must stay share-alike and credit OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Meta Relative Wealth Index: CC-BY-NC-4.0. The combined product is for non-commercial use unless the RWI fields are removed.
  • Copernicus (ERA5-Land, DEM): free including commercial use, with attribution to the Copernicus programme.
  • CC-BY-4.0 for GHSL, WorldPop, Gridfinder, geoBoundaries, ESA WorldCover and Open-Meteo; CDLA-Permissive-2.0 for Overture.
  • Survey microdata (DHS, World Bank MTF, NISR EICV5) and the Establishment Census tables are used under the providers' terms and never redistributed; the grid model publishes predictions, not cluster locations.
