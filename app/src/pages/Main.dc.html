Where Rwanda's buildings are, and what they would draw from the grid

6.4 million open building footprints, classified by use and matched to appliance-level RAMP load profiles. Climate, context and demand cover every building in the country; three contrasting sub-districts get a deep dive.

Area
Rwanda (national) Sub-district deep dives →
What this estimates. For each building: use type, a 24-hour load curve, peak load, annual energy, cooling category and a productive-use score.
Potential and expected demand. Potential: every building grid-connected, at 2022 appliance ownership (World Bank MTF), with household energy scaled to REG consumption. Expected: households × their probability of a grid connection, matched to EICV7 2024 per district.
How sure. Each page and section carries a status: Built = validated with external sources; Draft = no validation method yet; In progress = data being acquired or processed; Planned = future work. Most of the model is Draft until the Validation page is rebuilt.
