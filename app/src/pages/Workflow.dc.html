Reproducibility

  • One environment (pixi), one command per step; DuckDB over GeoParquet for the 6.4M-row tables.
  • Every download recorded in a content-addressed manifest: URL, sha256, size, licence, retrieval time.
  • Every table validated against a schema contract (pandera) before it is used; QA reports per table.
  • 93+ automated tests, including one that recomputes the dashboard's numbers from its own formulas.
  • RAMP runs seeded and initialised after users are added, so rebuilds are byte-identical.
