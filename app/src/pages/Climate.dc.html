The cooling-category rule

From each cell's 95th percentile of daily maximum (tmax p95) 2 m temperature over 2020–2025:

Low tmax p95 < 26 °C
Medium 26 to 29 °C
High 29 °C or more

A relative indicator of where fans and cooling become likely. Cooling degree-days above 22 °C are shown as a magnitude cross-check: a cell can be Medium on hot afternoons yet accumulate few degree-days, because nights in Rwanda are cool.

Independent validation: NASA POWER

Built

Hourly MERRA-2 temperatures from NASA POWER at four towns, against our ERA5-Land values (Copernicus, 2020–2025). Both agree that Kigali and Musanze have marginal cooling demand. The Rusizi/Bugarama valley is confirmed hot. In the eastern lowlands (11.6% of buildings sit in cells above 100 cooling degree-days a year) NASA POWER shows less heat, so that finding is provisional.

Sources: Muñoz-Sabater et al. (2021) ERA5-Land, Copernicus Climate Change Service; NASA Langley Research Center POWER Project.
